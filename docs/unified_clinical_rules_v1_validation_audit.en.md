# Unified Clinical Rules Layer v1 - Limitations and Acceptance Audit

Analysis objects:

- Rule implementation: `feature_extraction/ecgfeat/clinical_rules/` (Design document:
  `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-12-unified-clinical-ecg-rules-design.md`）
- Review samples: JS00002–JS00104, 99 items in total (JS00001 is processed separately and is not included in this statistics)
- Review method: Compare the final conclusion of the unified layer, the conclusion of the reference layer (Glasgow/DXL) and the original manual label one by one.
  And trace the systematic pattern back to the specific rule code to confirm whether it is a design trade-off or an implementation defect.

Processed individually.

## 1. Overall Conclusion (Initial audit on 2026-07-12, see update above for post-repair status)

The behavior of the unified rules layer at the v1 stage complies with the conservative principle of the design document that "missing evidence never constitutes a positive or normal".
But the price is that the vast majority of records stay at `incomplete`, which cannot yet be used as batch final diagnosis:

- 84/99 `incomplete`, 16/99 `technically_limited`, 0/99 get the complete `normal`
  or `abnormal`
- 100/100 hypertrophy field incomplete
- 98/100 Ischemia/infarction domain incomplete
- Among the 22 records with AF tag, unified layer final confirmation AF: 0 records
- Among the 4 RBBB tag records, RBBB confirmed by the unified layer: 0
- Among the 14 LVH label records, 2 have positive voltage on the same layer; another 6 have no LVH label but positive voltage
- Sinus bradycardia label 49/50, sinus bradycardia label 10/10 consistent with heart rate rules
- Ischemia screening rules are significantly more sensitive: 8 acute ST/occlusion patterns, 17 posterior wall ischemia patterns, and previous infarction
  Q wave pattern 4, short QT 13, long QT 5

Conclusion: **This is a safe baseline behavior deliberately chosen by v1, not a random implementation error** - heart rate judgment is stable
Domains that are available but require a chain of evidence (time bound measurements, template correlation, multiple sets of criteria complete) almost never give a positive
or negative conclusion. This is consistent with Chapter 12 of the design document "Non-Claims" (does not claim clinical validation, does not replace cardiology
(student review), but the current incomplete ratio is too high. This needs to be clearly recorded in the document to avoid being misused.
"Batch automatic diagnosis".

<a id="2-分域根因分析"></a>
## 2. Domain root cause analysis

<a id="21-心率-节律速率稳定可用"></a>
### 2.1 Heart rate/rhythm rate (stable, usable)

`rhythm.py:_adult_rate` only relies on `heart_rate_bpm` and `age_years`, which in most cases
Available in the recording, so sinus brady/sinus tachycardia judgments are highly consistent with manual labeling (49/50, 10/10). This is currently the only
Domains that can be considered "production ready".

<a id="22-afafl022-confirmed"></a>
### 2.2 AF/AFL (0/22 confirmed)

Root cause chain:

- `rhythm.py:76-99` (`CLIN-RHYTHM-AF-01`) requirements
  `rhythm.validated_qrst_subtraction` is true, otherwise it is directly `unavailable`.
- This logo comes from `atrial.py:build_qrst_subtracted_residual`, where
  `template_validated` (`atrial.py:610-615`) requirements: template correlation ≥0.85,
  R wave number ≥4, and the residual RMS ratio does not exceed the threshold.

On 10-second conventional recordings, especially in cases where the rhythm itself is irregular (AF/AFL), QRST template correlation is difficult
Stable up to 0.85, causing this validation condition to be systematically not met. This is because the upstream evidence the rule relies on is not strong enough.
The non-rule threshold is written incorrectly - but the objective result is that all AF/AFL tags cannot be confirmed by the unified layer and can only stay at
Reference layer (Glasgow/DXL).

<a id="23-rbbb-lbbb04-confirmed含-js00025-明显漏检"></a>
### 2.3 RBBB / LBBB (0/4 confirmed, including JS00025 obviously missed)

`conduction.py:_rbbb` / `_lbbb` both require beat-to-beat component time limits
(`r_prime_duration_ms`, `s_duration_ms`, `r_duration_ms`, etc.), these are from
Waveform segmentation of `delineate.py`. These time limits are often not measured on real signals (return `None`),
Once missing, `unavailable` will be determined, even if the QRS time limit itself has been significantly broadened - JS00025
(QRS 173 ms, label with LBBB) That's it: wide QRS has good evidence, but the unified layer is missing
The R wave timing in the lateral lead could not be confirmed.

This is exactly the conservative choice of Article 5.3 of the design document "It cannot be inferred based on amplitude alone, and it is unavailable without measurement".
The price is that the full BBB is almost never confirmed.

<a id="24-肥大hypertrophy100100-不完整"></a>
### 2.4 hypertrophy (100/100 incomplete)

`hypertrophy.py:_adult_lvh` (approximately lines 94-115) requires Cornell voltage, Cornell
A negative conclusion is allowed only if all four sets of standard measurement values of product, Sokolow-Lyon, and R-aVL exist;
As long as one group is missing, even if the remaining three groups of data are complete and all are negative, the overall result is still `unavailable`.
Similarly, `_adult_rvh` and `_adult_atrial` (RAE/LAE) all rely on time-limited measurements.
(Such as `r_prime_duration_ms`, `p_terminal_duration_ms`). This explains why
The fat fields of 100 records are all incomplete - not a rule error, but "partial data cannot be equated to negative"
Evidence" design choice will almost certainly lead to `unavailable` given the current measurement success rate.

Only 2 of the 14 LVH label records were positive for voltage on the same layer, and the other 6 records had no LVH label but were positive for voltage.
——This is consistent with the positioning of Design Document 5.5 "Reported voltage standard hits are not equivalent to anatomical ventricular hypertrophy",
Voltage positivity and clinical LVH labeling are not the same thing per se and cannot directly corroborate each other.

<a id="25-st-q-波-缺血-梗死模式偏敏感"></a>
### 2.5 ST / Q wave / ischemia-infarction mode (more sensitive)

Design document 5.6 itself acknowledges that screening for posterior wall ischemia and acute ST/occlusion is a "suggestive" level and does not
RBBB, wide QRS, LVH, past ECG, symptoms, and dynamic changes for specific suppression (V7-V9 posterior wall verification,
Sgarbossa path etc. still to be improved). The 2023 ESC ACS guidelines also clearly point out that ST elevation in RBBB
The presence or absence cannot be reliably evaluated. Therefore, the current rules have high hit rate and low specificity, which is in line with expectations but needs to be used on the user side.
Clearly marked "screening, not diagnostic".

<a id="4-结论与限制声明"></a>
## 4. Conclusion and Limitations

- Heart rate/rhythm rate (sinus brady/sinus tachycardia) is currently the only field that can be considered production-ready.
- AF/AFL, RBBB/LBBB, the four groups of hypertrophy criteria, and the ischemia/infarction domain all rely on the upstream beat-to-beat time limit which is not yet stable.
  Measurement or template correlation verification, the v1 phase systematically falls on `unavailable`/`incomplete`, which is
  The expected cost of the "missing evidence does not constitute a positive or normal" design principle is not a writing error in the rule threshold.
- The ischemia/ST screening rule itself implements error-free, but specifically suppresses, ESC/UDMI adjacent lead thresholds
  (RBBB, wide QRS, LVH, past ECG, symptom, dynamic ECG) have not yet been connected, the hit should be regarded as
  "screening" prompts and cannot be used as an independent diagnostic conclusion.
- In these dependency chains (especially the beat-to-beat time limit measurement success rate of `delineate.py`,
  Until the QRST template correlation for `atrial.py` is improved, the unified layer is not suitable to be used directly as a batch
  Automated final diagnostic output should continue to require manual review and be clearly marked on the report/application side
  The meaning of `incomplete`/`technically_limited`.

## 5. Optional follow-up direction (not implemented in this round)

- Quantify upstream measurement failure rate: statistics on 100 samples
  `r_prime_duration_ms`/`s_duration_ms`/`r_duration_ms`/`template_correlation`
  Wait for the missing proportion of key fields to distinguish between two types of reasons: "really unmeasurable" and "delineate.py can be improved".
- For typical missed cases such as JS00025, separately check the R wave of the lateral lead of `delineate.py`
  Reason for failure of time limit extraction.
- Evaluate whether it is necessary to design for some rules (such as AF template correlation threshold, LVH four sets of standard requirements)
  More fine-grained "partial evidence + confidence downgrade" output instead of binary
  matched/unavailable.
